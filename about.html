Our Story

Rooted in Lanna Heritage

A home fragrance brand inspired by Thai and Lanna cultural heritage, blended with the serenity of nature in Northern Thailand.

Mission & Vision

Sharing the Beauty of
Thai Lanna Heritage

Poetiq is inspired by the delicate artistry of Lanna pattern and the serene nature of Northern Thailand. Through refined home fragrance creations, the brand shares the beauty of Thai Lanna heritage with people around the world, while reflecting Thailand's cultural wisdom and roots.

Each fragrance is crafted around the highland forests of Chiang Mai, the oolong tea plantations of Chiang Rai, and the lantern festival of Sukhothai — inviting people to experience the roots of Lanna culture, the richness of nature, and the Thai way of life.

The Symbol

Phor Phan & Ror Ruea,
Woven Together

The Poetiq mark is drawn from two Lanna characters — "Phor Phan" (พ) and "Ror Ruea" (ร) — adapted and interlaced with traditional Lanna woven textile patterns into a single, connected form. The trademark has been registered with Thailand's Department of Intellectual Property.

Community & Sustainability

Made With, Not Just For

Poetiq collaborates with local communities to develop artisan skills in provincial areas, creating additional income alongside the production process. Materials and packaging are chosen to be safe for health and the environment — avoiding plastic and minimising waste through less-waste production.

The Forest Bathing Collection

Every scent in the collection is built around a place — a memory of Northern Thailand you can bring home.
